Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

The short version: Cre8Post runs entirely on your own Windows PC. The developer does not run any server for it and does not collect, see, store, sell or share your data. Your videos, captions and account sign-ins stay on your computer and are sent only to the platforms you choose to post to.

Who we are

Cre8Post ("the app") is a desktop application made by Anthony, doing business as GenX CountryBoy, in Oregon, USA ("we", "us"). You can reach us at antoneogenxcountryboy@gmail.com.

How the app works

Cre8Post is installed and runs on your computer. When you schedule a post, the app stores the details on your computer. At the scheduled time, it uploads the video directly from your computer to each platform you selected, using that platform's official API. There is no Cre8Post server in between, and we never receive a copy of your content or account information.

We do not collect your passwords. You sign in on each platform's own official login page, and the platform gives the app a token that you can revoke at any time.

The app has no analytics, tracking, advertising or telemetry, and it does not contact any server operated by us.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade any data.
  • We do not use your data for advertising or to train AI models.
  • We do not read your messages, followers, comments, analytics or any content other than what you choose to post.
  • We do not transfer your data to anyone. Data moves only between your computer and the platforms you post to.

Third-party platforms

When you post, your content is sent to the platforms you selected, and their own terms and privacy policies apply to it there:

  • TikTok: Privacy Policy and Terms of Service
  • YouTube: YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy
  • Instagram and Facebook: Meta Privacy Policy and Meta Terms

Google and YouTube data

Cre8Post uses YouTube API Services. By connecting YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

The app requests only the youtube.upload permission, which it uses solely to upload the videos you schedule to your channel, with the title, description and visibility you set.

Cre8Post's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can revoke Cre8Post's access to your Google account at any time at Google security settings.

TikTok data

Cre8Post uses TikTok's Login Kit and Content Posting API with the user.info.basic, video.publish and video.upload scopes. It reads your basic profile and the posting options TikTok allows for your account, and publishes only the videos you schedule, with the caption and privacy level you choose. For posts you mark as affiliate, it uses video.upload to send the video to your TikTok inbox as a draft instead, so you can add products and finish the post yourself in the TikTok app. You can disconnect in the app, or revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.

Meta (Instagram and Facebook) data

Cre8Post uses the Meta Graph API to list the Facebook Pages you manage and their linked Instagram professional accounts, and to publish the videos you schedule as Instagram Reels and Facebook Page videos. You can disconnect in the app, or remove Cre8Post in Facebook under Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations. See our Data Deletion page for full instructions.

Keeping, deleting and controlling your data

All data stays on your computer until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete any scheduled or past post from the app's queue.
  • Disconnect any platform in the app's Settings, which deletes that platform's stored token.
  • Delete everything by uninstalling the app and deleting the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cre8Post.

Content already published to a platform is controlled by that platform. Delete it there if you want it removed.

Security

Tokens and settings are stored in your Windows user profile, outside synced cloud folders, and the app only accepts connections from your own computer. Keep your computer and Windows account secure, since anyone with access to your Windows account could use the app.

Children

Cre8Post is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly handle data from them. Each platform also has its own minimum age.
