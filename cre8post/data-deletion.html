Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated October 18, 2026

Cre8Post does not store any of your data on our servers, because it doesn't have any. Everything the app keeps is on your own computer, so you can delete it yourself at any time.

Delete your data from the app

  1. Open Cre8Post, go to Settings, and click Disconnect on each connected platform. This deletes that platform's stored access token.
  2. To remove everything, quit Cre8Post from its tray icon, then delete the folder %LOCALAPPDATA%\Cre8Post (paste that into the File Explorer address bar). This removes your schedule, post history, settings and all tokens.
  3. Uninstall or delete the app.

Revoke access on each platform

  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook → Settings and privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites) → remove Cre8Post.
  • YouTube / Google: visit Google security settings and remove Cre8Post.
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Profile → Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions → remove Cre8Post.

Content you already posted

Videos published to a platform live on that platform. Delete them there if you want them removed.
