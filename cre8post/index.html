Schedule once. Post everywhere.

Cre8Post is a Windows desktop app that posts your videos to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and Facebook on the day and time you pick. It runs on your own PC, with no monthly fee and no middleman server.

How it works

Cre8Post runs quietly in the Windows system tray and checks its queue every few seconds.

  1. Connect your accounts. You sign in to each platform through that platform's own official sign-in page. Cre8Post never sees your passwords.
  2. Schedule your videos. Pick videos from your folder, add a caption, tick the platforms, and choose a date and time.
  3. Cre8Post posts them. At the scheduled time, the app uploads the video directly from your PC to each platform using that platform's official API.
  4. Check the result. The queue shows what posted, what failed and why, with links to the live posts.

Platforms and permissions

Cre8Post only asks for what it needs to publish videos you choose to post. It does not read your messages, followers, or analytics.

TikTok Instagram YouTube Facebook

TikTok

Basic profile info and permission to publish videos you schedule (Content Posting API). Your privacy setting choice is respected on every post.

YouTube

Permission to upload videos to your channel (YouTube Data API). Nothing else on your Google account is accessed.

Instagram and Facebook

Permission to publish Reels to your Instagram professional account and videos to a Facebook Page you manage (Meta Graph API).
